Delete your RiseUp League account

For the RiseUp Padel League app (iOS and Android) and riseup-league.com, by Smart Wave

You can ask us to delete your RiseUp League account and the personal data linked to it at any time. You don't need the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below, or email info@swt-iq.com with the subject "Delete my RiseUp League account".
  2. Include the phone number and name on your account so we can find it. Send the request from an email address you can be reached at.
  3. We may contact you on that phone number to confirm the request is really yours.
  4. We delete the account and email you to confirm, within 30 days.
Request account deletion

If the button doesn't open your email app, write to info@swt-iq.com yourself, or call +964 784 440 0555 (Sun–Thu, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM).

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Your phone number and password
  • Your name and profile photo
  • City, dominant hand, court side and Instagram link
  • Your sign-in sessions on every device
  • Your season pass progress, prizes and partner invites

Kept

  • Scores of matches you played, without your name or photo, so other players' results and leaderboards stay correct
  • Anything we are required to keep by law

As soon as we process your request, your account is removed from the app and you are signed out everywhere. Your personal data is then permanently erased within 30 days. You can't undo this; to play again you will need to sign up for a new account.
