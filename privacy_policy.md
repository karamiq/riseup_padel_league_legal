# RiseUp League Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This policy explains what personal data the RiseUp League app (iOS and Android) and the website https://riseup-league.com collect about you, why, who can see it and what you can do about it.

RiseUp League is run by **Smart Wave** ("we", "us"), Al-Yarmook, Baghdad, Iraq. For any privacy question or request, write to **info@swt-iq.com**.

## 1. What we collect

### When you sign up
- **Phone number.** Your login name. We use it to identify your account and to let league staff contact you about the league.
- **Password.** Stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt). Nobody, including us, can read it.
- **Name** and **profile photo.** A photo is required to sign up. You choose it from your gallery or take it with the camera, and crop it before it is sent. The app asks for camera and photo access only for this, and only when you tap to pick a photo.
- **City**, **preferred court side**, **dominant hand** and the **group** (level) you start in.

New accounts wait for approval by league staff before they can log in.

### What you can add later
- An **Instagram link** on your profile (optional).
- Changes to your name, photo, city, hand or court side.

### What is created while you play
- The sessions you join or leave, the partners you invite or accept, the matches you play and their results.
- Your ELO rating, group, season stats, leaderboard positions, season pass progress, and the prizes you earn, claim and use.
- A record of some actions on your account (for example claiming or using a prize), with the time and your user ID, so staff can resolve disputes.

### Sign-in data
- When you log in we create a **sign-in session** with a random device ID and an expiry date (30 days, renewed while you use the app). It is deleted when you log out.
- **Website only:** if you allow notifications in your browser, the website stores a Firebase Cloud Messaging token with your sign-in session so we can send you notifications. The mobile app does not collect one.

### What we do not collect
The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. It does not read your location, contacts or other files, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

## 2. Data kept on your device
The app stores on your phone: your sign-in tokens and user ID, your app settings (such as language and theme), the season you last selected and a cached list of groups. Deleting the app or logging out removes the sign-in data.

The website stores the same kind of data in cookies and browser storage (sign-in tokens, your user ID, settings, and small flags such as whether you closed a banner). These are needed for the site to work; we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 3. Who can see your data

**Other players and visitors.** RiseUp League is a public league. Your name, profile photo, city, group, dominant hand, court side, Instagram link (if you added one), ELO, stats, match results, leaderboard positions and the sessions you take part in can be seen by other players and by visitors browsing in guest mode.

**League staff.** Admins can see and manage all accounts, including phone numbers, to approve sign-ups, run sessions, record results and hand out prizes.

**Service providers.** We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use these providers to run the service:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A cloud hosting provider | All account and league data | Hosting our servers and database |
| Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Notification token and the notification content | Sending notifications to the website (and to the app if push notifications are added later) |
| Google Fonts | Your device's IP address when the app downloads its fonts | Displaying the app's fonts |

Tapping a link in the app (Instagram, WhatsApp community, a court's map location, sharing a session) opens another app or website, which handles your data under its own policy.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it
- Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity, or when you log out.
- Your account and play history are kept while your account is active, because league results, leaderboards and season history depend on them.
- When an account is deleted it is removed from the app and all of its sign-in sessions are ended. Its personal data is permanently erased within 30 days of deletion.

## 5. Your choices and rights
- **See and edit** your profile data at any time on the Profile screen.
- **Log out** to end your sign-in session on that device.
- **Delete your account** by writing to info@swt-iq.com with the phone number on your account. We will delete it and confirm within 30 days. See [how to delete your account](https://karamiq.github.io/rise_padel_league_privacy_policy/delete-account.html).
- **Ask for a copy** of your data or object to how we use it by writing to info@swt-iq.com.
- **Notifications** on the website can be turned off in your browser settings at any time.

## 6. Security
Connections to our servers use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, and access tokens expire after 30 minutes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

## 7. Children
RiseUp League is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data; if you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

## 8. Changes to this policy
We will post any change on this page and update the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app or on the website.
